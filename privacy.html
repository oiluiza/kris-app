Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Kris is a private personal assistant used by a single person, its owner, on their own computer.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorization, Kris accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets) through Google APIs, only to read and organize that data at the owner's request.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own computer.
  • Kris has no server and does not collect, sell, share or transfer data to third parties.
  • Data is not used for advertising or to train models.
  • The authorization token is stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Kris's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
